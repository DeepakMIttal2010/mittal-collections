# User Manual (Customer Guide)

**Site:** https://www.mittalcollections.com
**Document version:** 1.2
**Last updated:** 2026-08-25

This guide walks through the site as a shopper. For store-management
instructions, see `ADMIN_MANUAL.md`.

## 1. Browsing & Search

- Use the top navigation to browse a category (Doormats, Bedsheets,
  Towels, Curtains, Pillows, Cushions, Blankets, Hotel Linen, and
  more), or a subcategory within it. The top few categories show
  directly in the nav with a subcategory flyout; the rest are grouped
  under a **More** dropdown so the nav bar doesn't get overcrowded.
  Use the sort/filter controls on a category page to narrow by price
  or view newest-first.
- Type in the header search bar for live suggestions as you type. The
  search tolerates minor typos. Use the **All ▾** dropdown next to the
  search bar to scope your search to one category before typing (e.g.
  search only within Bedsheets). If a search returns nothing, the
  results page suggests categories to browse instead.
- Tap the microphone icon in the search bar to search by voice
  (browser permission required).
- **New Arrivals** (`/new-arrivals`) and **Top Trending** (`/trending`)
  each have their own page, organized category by category, alongside
  the homepage strips of the same name — click "Show More" under a
  category's section to load more without leaving the page.
- **Clearance Sale** (`/clearance-sale`): a dedicated page (and
  homepage carousel) that automatically pulls together every category
  with a significant markdown (35%+ off) — it updates on its own as
  discounts change, it isn't a hand-picked list.
- "You may also like" / "Compare with similar products" on a product
  page are further ways to discover more.
- The header shows "Deliver to [Name] / [City] [Pincode]" — your saved
  default address if you're logged in, or a location guess based on
  your connection if not. If you're logged in and have more than one
  saved address, click it to open a quick dropdown and switch your
  delivery address without leaving the page.
- **EN | हिं** in the header switches the site's static text (and any
  product whose seller has added a Hindi name/description) between
  English and Hindi. Your choice is remembered on your device.

## 2. Product Pages

Each product page shows: photo gallery (pinch/click to zoom), video (if
available), price, stock status, description (long descriptions
collapse behind a "See more" toggle), and — where the seller has
filled them in — Fabric, Size, GSM, Wash Care, Brand, Country of
Origin, and a **What's Included** line (e.g. "Set of 5 Cushion
Covers") if the product is sold as a set. A row of trust badges below
the price shows whether Cash on Delivery, Secure Payment, and a
genuine-product guarantee apply (always), plus this specific product's
real return window — some products are marked non-returnable, so
check this badge rather than assuming every product returns the same
way.

- **Sizes**: products that come in more than one size at different
  prices (e.g. Curtains as 7x4 or 9x4) show a size picker — pick a
  size to see its own price and stock before adding to cart. Two
  different sizes of the same product can sit in your cart at once as
  separate lines.
- **Colour notice**: if a product's actual colour can vary from photo
  to photo (a common case with home-textile dye lots), the page shows
  a highlighted note asking you to contact the store first if you need
  a specific colour.
- **Complete the Look**: on some categories, buying items from two
  paired categories together (e.g. a Bedsheet + Cushion Covers)
  automatically unlocks an extra discount at checkout — no coupon code
  needed. Look for the "Complete the Look" banner on the product or
  category page; your cart and checkout will show exactly which items
  qualified and the discount applied.

Below that:
- **Loyalty points preview** — how many points you'll earn if you buy this now.
- **Reviews** — real customer ratings and comments.
- **Questions & Answers** — ask the seller a question, or read answers to others'.
- **Compare with similar products** — an automatic side-by-side table against similar items in the same category.

Buttons: **Add to Cart**, **Buy it now** (skips straight to checkout),
and **Order on WhatsApp** (opens a WhatsApp chat pre-filled with this
product — disabled if it's out of stock). If a product is out of
stock, you can leave your email to be notified the moment it's back.

## 3. Cart & Checkout

1. Add items via any product card, product page, or quick view (eye
   icon on a product card opens a fast preview without leaving the
   page).
2. Open the cart via the cart icon (slide-out drawer) or the full
   `/cart` page. Both show your subtotal, current delivery fee, how
   many loyalty points this order will earn, and — if your cart
   qualifies — a "Complete the Look" bundle discount, with a
   click-to-expand breakdown of which items qualified.
3. **Delivery fee**: free above a store-set order value; below that, a
   small fee that gets lower the closer your order is to the free
   threshold.
4. Click **Proceed to Checkout**. You'll be asked to log in if you
   aren't already (you're returned to checkout automatically after).
5. Choose or add a delivery address.
6. Apply a coupon code if you have one — first-time customers are
   automatically shown an eligible welcome coupon.
7. If you have loyalty points, a slider lets you redeem them for a
   discount (up to a store-set % of the order).
8. Choose your payment method — **Razorpay (online payment)** is
   selected by default; switch to **Cash on Delivery** if you'd rather
   pay on arrival. Choosing COD adds a small COD handling charge to
   your total (shown as its own line before you confirm) to cover the
   courier's real cash-collection fee — this doesn't apply if you pay
   online. Then **Place Order**.
   - With Razorpay selected, a secure payment window opens for you to
     pay by card, UPI, netbanking, or wallet. If you close the payment
     window or the payment fails, your order isn't lost — it's saved
     as Pending/unpaid and you can complete payment later from **My
     Orders**.
9. You'll get an order-confirmation email as soon as your order is
   placed — you don't have to wait for the store to update its status
   to know it went through.

## 4. Your Account

Creating a new account shows a "Registration Successful" popup with
your welcome coupon code and a summary of loyalty/referral/delivery
benefits, and takes you straight to your Account page.

Sign in, then visit **Account** for:
- **Your Orders** — order history with a visual status timeline per
  order (Ordered → Processing → Shipped → Delivered, each step with
  its real date/time, or a distinct Cancelled state), a full price
  breakdown (items, delivery fee, COD charge if applicable, coupon
  discount, bundle discount, loyalty points redeemed, down to the
  final total), a **Return** button on each eligible delivered item
  (only shown within that item's return window), and a **Get Product
  Support** link that opens a support ticket pre-linked to that order.
  You'll also get an email and a bell notification whenever an order's
  status changes, and about 8 days after delivery you may get a
  follow-up email asking for a review of what you bought.
- **Returns** (`/returns`) — track every return you've requested,
  through Requested → Approved/Rejected → Picked Up → Refunded.
- **Support Tickets** (`/tickets`) — raise a support request and reply
  in a message thread with the store; you'll be notified (email + bell)
  whenever they reply.
- **Wishlist** — save products for later.
- **Your Addresses** — manage saved delivery addresses.
- **Edit Profile** / **Change Password**.
- **Loyalty points balance** and **full points history** (every earn,
  redeem, refund, or expiry, with running balance).
- **Referral program** — your personal referral link/code, plus a
  one-tap WhatsApp share button. When someone you referred completes
  their first delivered order, you both get bonus points.

> If you try to open any account page without being logged in, you're
> sent to the login page and brought right back afterward — nothing is
> silently hidden or blank.

## 5. Notifications

The bell icon in the header (once you're logged in) shows a count of
unread updates — order status changes, replies on your support
tickets, return status updates, back-in-stock alerts you subscribed
to, and loyalty points expiry. Click one to jump straight to the
relevant page, or open **View all notifications** for the full
history at `/notifications`.

## 6. Comparing Products

Click the ⇄ icon on any product card or quick-view popup to add it to
your comparison list (up to 4 at once). A floating bar appears at the
bottom of the screen — tap **Compare** to see them side by side
(price, rating, specs, availability), with an Add to Cart button for
each.

## 7. Guides & Tools

- **Guides & Ideas** (`/articles`) — buying guides and styling tips.
- **Curtain Size Calculator** — enter your window measurements and
  preferences to get the exact rod length, fabric width, and curtain
  length to buy, plus the closest standard size available.

## 8. On Mobile

- A fixed bottom tab bar (Home / Categories / Cart / Account) stays on
  screen as you browse, so the essentials are always one tap away.
- If your browser supports it and the app isn't already installed, an
  **Install App** icon appears in the header — tap it to add Mittal
  Collections to your home screen like a native app. On iOS Safari
  (which doesn't support one-tap install), tapping it shows you how to
  add it manually via the Share menu.

## 9. Getting Help

- **WhatsApp** — the green floating button (bottom-left, every page)
  opens a chat with the store.
- **Support Tickets** (`/tickets`) — raise a ticket for anything that
  needs back-and-forth (order issue, product question, complaint) and
  get replies in-thread, with an email + bell notification each time.
- **Contact page** — a message form.
- **FAQ** — common questions (shipping, COD, returns, delivery time,
  loyalty points) are answered right on the homepage.
